Our Story

A Legacy Since 1935

Nestled on Nehru Road — the heart of Darjeeling — Glenary's has been a beloved institution for nearly a century. Founded during the British Raj, it began as a modest bakery and grew into one of the most iconic dining destinations in the Eastern Himalayas.

The aroma of freshly baked bread drifting down The Mall, the warmth of a wood-panelled dining room, and the sight of snow-capped peaks through the windows — this is Glenary's. A place where time slows down and every meal becomes a memory.
